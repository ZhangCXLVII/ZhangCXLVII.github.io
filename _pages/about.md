Previously, I received B.Eng in artificial intelligence from South China Normal University, advised by Prof. Lihua Cai. I am grateful to receive advice from Prof. Daochang Liu when I did research at School of Physics, Mathematics and Computing, The University of Western Australia.

My research focuses on how to understand the world through vision. This broadly includes world model, video generation, embodied intelligence and multimodal learning.
